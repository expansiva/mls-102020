Assess every published molecule group for every supplied organism/device need. Call
submitD2MoleculeGroups once. Use the published purpose, actor intent, available inputs,
outputs, interactions and accessibility needs. Give a specific reason for each relevant
or discarded group. Do not infer a role from a tag or group name. An empty catalog is
different from a catalog with no suitable group. This pass selects groups for deeper
reading; the following pass chooses preferred and optional alternative candidates.
